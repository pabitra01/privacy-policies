Privacy Policy

ClassMate App | Last updated: November 21, 2026

Your study data stays on your device. ClassMate has no account and no server of its own. Your timetable, attendance, homework, exams, notes and grades never leave your phone unless you export them yourself. ClassMate is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show those ads (see section 4).

1. Information Stored on Your Device

ClassMate stores the following locally on your device only. We (the developer) never receive it:

  • Subjects, class timetable and holidays
  • Attendance records and extra classes
  • Homework, exams and their reminders
  • Personal notes and grade records
  • Study material file references (file links only; files are not copied or uploaded)
  • Focus timer sessions and study time
  • Your name (if you enter one), app preferences and settings
  • Ad settings such as when an ad was last shown and when an ad-free period ends

2. Data Storage and Backups

Study data is kept in a local database on your device. There is no cloud storage or synchronization. If you create a backup or export a PDF or CSV file, it is saved or shared only where you choose (for example your Files app or a chat app), and what happens to it there is up to you.

3. Data Sharing

We do not collect, sell or share your study data. The only data that leaves your device is the device and usage data that Google's advertising service collects to show ads, described below.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ClassMate shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location
  • Device information such as model, Android version and language
  • Interactions with ads (for example views and taps) and app performance and diagnostic data

This data is sent to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ads in ClassMate are limited to content Google rates as suitable for teens.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in a region where the law requires it, ClassMate asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in ClassMate → Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

Your ad controls

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Watch one short video on the More screen to turn off all ads for 24 hours.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads and the consent form. Your study data is never sent over the internet.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and start on boot: to remind you about classes, homework and exams, and to keep reminders working after a restart.
  • Vibration: for reminder and focus timer alerts.

6. File Access

The Study Materials feature lets you pick files using Android's built-in file picker. The app stores only a link to the file (its URI). It does not copy, upload or change your files.

7. Children's Privacy

ClassMate is intended for students aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, you can delete all of its data as described in section 8, and contact us with any questions.

8. Data Deletion

Because your study data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing the app data in Android Settings → Apps → ClassMate → Storage → Clear data
  • Uninstalling the app (this permanently removes all data)

Data collected by Google for advertising is managed by Google. You can reset your advertising ID as described in section 4, or use My Ad Center to manage ad personalization for your Google account.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
